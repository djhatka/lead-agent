How to delete your data

For {{ s.brand }} users, and for people who connected Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp or Google to it.

If you have a {{ s.brand }} account

  1. Log in at {{ s.app_url }}.
  2. Optional: Settings → Download my data, to keep a copy.
  3. Settings → Delete account, then type your password to confirm.

Your business, customers, messages, posts, photos, reviews and connected-account keys are deleted from the live service immediately, and from backups within 30 days. We keep only payment records, which tax law requires for 8 years.

If you only want to disconnect a platform

Settings → Connections → Disconnect. We delete that platform's keys at once. You can also remove our app from your Facebook or Instagram settings (Settings → Apps and websites) or your Google account (myaccount.google.com/permissions).

If you are a customer of a business that uses {{ s.brand }}

Your enquiry belongs to that business. Ask them to delete it, or write to us at {{ s.email_html | safe }} with the business's name and your phone number, and we will pass it on and help them do it within 30 days.

Need help?

Write to our grievance officer, {{ s.grievance_html | safe }}.
