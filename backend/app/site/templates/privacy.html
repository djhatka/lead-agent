Privacy policy

Last updated {{ s.updated }}

This policy explains what personal data {{ s.company }} ("we") collects through {{ s.brand }}, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and how you can control it. It is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules, and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

1. Two kinds of data

  • Data about you, the business owner or staff member using {{ s.brand }}. For this data we decide how it is used (we are the "data fiduciary").
  • Data about your customers that you bring into the service: enquiries, messages, reviews and contact details. You decide how it is used; we process it only on your behalf and on your instructions (we are your "data processor"). Your customers should contact you about it; we will help you answer them.

2. What we collect, and why

DataWhy we need it
Name, email, phone, password (stored only as a scrambled one-way code)To create your account, log you in and contact you about it
Business details: name, industry, city, area, services, brand voice, review linkTo run the service and tailor AI suggestions to your business
Your customers' enquiries: names, phone numbers, Instagram/Facebook IDs, messages, lead emails, imported lists, reviewsTo show them in your inbox, score them, send your replies and prepare reports (on your behalf)
Connected accounts: access keys for WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and Google (stored encrypted)To receive messages, send your replies, publish approved posts and read reviews and post numbers
Photos and videos you upload (location data is removed on upload)To publish the posts you approve
Billing: plan, payment ID, amount, dates. Card/UPI details are handled by Razorpay; we never see or store themTo bill you, send receipts and keep records the law requires
Technical data: IP address, browser, pages used, error logsTo keep the service secure, prevent misuse and fix problems

We do not sell personal data, do not use it for advertising, and do not use your customers' data to train AI models. We use only what is needed for the purposes above.

3. Consent

By signing up you agree to us using your data as described here. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account (Settings → Delete account) or writing to us; this stops the service and leads to deletion as set out below. Withdrawal does not affect processing done before it.

4. Who we share data with

Only with service providers who help us run {{ s.brand }}, under contracts that require them to protect it, and only as needed:

  • Hosting and storage: our cloud server and database provider.
  • AI processing: Anthropic (the Claude AI models), to score enquiries and draft text. Under Anthropic's commercial terms for its API, this data is not used to train its models.
  • Platforms you connect: Meta (WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook) and Google, to receive and send messages, publish posts and read reviews.
  • Payments: Razorpay. Email delivery: our email provider. Incoming lead emails: Cloudflare.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Some of these providers may process data outside India. We only use providers with strong security commitments, and transfers follow Indian law.

Google user data: {{ s.brand }}'s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google Business Profile data only to show and reply to your reviews and publish your approved posts.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account and customer data: while your account is active. If you delete your account, it is erased within 30 days (immediately from the live service; backups roll over within 30 days).
  • If your trial or plan ends and you don't return, we keep your data for up to 12 months so you can come back. Before deleting it we email you at least 48 hours in advance.
  • Payment records: 8 years, because Indian tax law requires it.
  • Security logs: 1 year, as the DPDP Rules require.

6. How we protect it

HTTPS everywhere; passwords stored only as one-way codes; platform keys encrypted; each business can only see its own data; staff access is limited; daily backups. If a breach affecting your personal data happens, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India without delay, as the law requires.

7. Your rights

  • Access and export: download all your data any time (Settings → Download my data).
  • Correction: edit your details in Settings, or ask us.
  • Erasure: delete your account (Settings → Delete account) or see how to delete your data.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to.
  • Complain to our grievance officer (below). If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Cookies

We use only what is needed to keep you logged in and the site working. No advertising or tracking cookies.

9. Children

{{ s.brand }} is for businesses and is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect children's data.
