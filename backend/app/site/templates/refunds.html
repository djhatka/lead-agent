Cancellation and refund policy

Last updated {{ s.updated }}

Try before you pay

Every business starts with a {{ s.trial_days }}-day free trial with all {{ s.trial_name }} features and no card needed, so you can decide before paying anything.

Cancelling

  • Cancel any time from Billing in your dashboard, or by writing to {{ s.email_html | safe }}.
  • Your plan stops renewing straight away, and you keep full access until the end of the month you have already paid for.
  • There are no cancellation fees.

Refunds

  • Because you can try everything free first, monthly payments are not refunded for partly used months.
  • We do refund: payments taken by mistake (for example a double charge, or a renewal after you cancelled), and the unused part of a month if we remove a major feature you paid for or the service is unavailable for more than 3 days in a row because of our fault.
  • To ask for a refund, write to {{ s.email_html | safe }} within 30 days of the payment, with the payment ID from your receipt.
  • Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method (card, UPI or bank) through Razorpay within 5–7 working days. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

Changing plans

You can move to another plan any time. The new plan starts when you pay for it, and your old plan stops renewing.
